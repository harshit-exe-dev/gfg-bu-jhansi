Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This page explains what information the GeeksforGeeks Campus Body website (Bundelkhand University, Jhansi — Chapter 02) collects, why, and what control you have over it. The short version: we collect as little as possible, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

What we collect

  • Join form — when you register with the chapter, we store the name, branch, and year you enter. That is all the form asks for.
  • Gallery photos — photos uploaded to the chapter gallery by the chapter team.
  • Visit counts — the site counts page views in aggregate to understand which sections students use. These counts are not linked to your identity.

Where it is stored

Join-form details are stored in a MongoDB Atlas database, and gallery photos are stored on Cloudinary, a media hosting service. Both are accessed only by the chapter's site administrator.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies, and uses no third-party analytics or advertising services. The admin area (used only by the chapter team) keeps a temporary sign-in token in your browser's session storage — it disappears when the tab is closed.

Who sees your details

Your join-form details are visible only to the chapter team for running chapter activities such as workshops and events. They are never published on this site and never shared with anyone outside the chapter team, unless required by law.

Your control

If you joined through the form and want your details removed, contact the chapter team through the chapter's LinkedIn or Instagram pages linked in the footer of the homepage, and we will delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
